Core Technologies

Advanced technologies powering our products

Ultra Fast Charging

Our proprietary fast charging technology supports PD3.0/QC4.0 protocols, delivering up to 100W charging power for laptops and mobile devices. Charge your device 50% in just 30 minutes.

9-Layer Safety System

Comprehensive intelligent safety protection including overcharge, over-discharge, over-voltage, short circuit, temperature control, and more. Your safety is our top priority.

High-Density Battery

Advanced lithium-ion battery technology with high energy density, providing more power in a compact form factor. Premium battery cells from top-tier suppliers.

Wireless Charging

Qi-certified wireless charging technology supporting 15W fast wireless charging for compatible devices. Foreign object detection for enhanced safety.

Smart IC Chip

Custom-designed smart chip that automatically detects device requirements and optimizes charging efficiency. Intelligent power distribution for multiple devices.

Thermal Management

Advanced thermal dissipation system that keeps the device cool during high-power charging. Optimized airflow and heat sink design for reliable performance.
