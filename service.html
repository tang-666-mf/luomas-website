FAQ

Common questions about our products and services

How long does the power bank take to fully charge?

Charging time varies depending on the capacity and charging method. A 10000mAh power bank takes approximately 4-6 hours with a 20W charger. Larger capacity models may take longer. We recommend using the original charging cable and adapter for optimal performance.

Is it safe to charge my laptop with a power bank?

Yes, our high-capacity power banks (20000mAh and above) support PD/QC fast charging and can safely charge laptops. Please check the product specifications for compatibility. Models with 65W or higher output are recommended for laptop charging.

How do I check the remaining battery level?

Most Luomas power banks have LED indicators that show the remaining battery level. Press the power button to activate the display. Each LED typically represents 25% of the battery capacity. Some models also feature a digital display showing exact percentage.

What should I do if my power bank is not working?

First, try charging the power bank for at least 30 minutes. If it still doesn't work, check the charging cable and adapter. Try a different cable or adapter to isolate the issue. If the problem persists, contact our support team with your order information for warranty service.

Can I carry a power bank on an airplane?

Yes, power banks are allowed in carry-on luggage. However, most airlines restrict power banks above 100Wh (approximately 27000mAh). Power banks must be carried in carry-on luggage only, not checked baggage. Please check with your airline for specific regulations before traveling.
