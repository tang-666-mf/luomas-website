Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products

How long does a Romoss power bank take to charge?
Charging time varies depending on the capacity and input method. A 10000mAh power bank takes approximately 4-6 hours with a 2A charger. Fast-charging models can charge from 0-50% in about 30 minutes.
How many times can I charge my phone with a Romoss power bank?
This depends on your phone's battery capacity. For example, a 20000mAh power bank can charge an iPhone 14 (3274mAh) approximately 5 times, considering typical conversion efficiency of about 85%.
Is it safe to leave my power bank charging overnight?
Yes, all Romoss power banks feature intelligent charging protection that automatically stops charging once full. Our 9-layer safety protection system ensures safe charging at all times.
What is the warranty period for Romoss products?
All Romoss power banks come with a 12-month warranty from the date of purchase. This covers manufacturing defects but not accidental damage or misuse.
Can I take my Romoss power bank on a plane?
Yes, Romoss power banks are safe for air travel. Most airlines allow power banks up to 100Wh (typically 27000mAh) in carry-on luggage. Always check with your airline for specific regulations.
