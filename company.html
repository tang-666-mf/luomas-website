Our Story

Founded in 2012, Luomas has grown from a small startup into a global leader in mobile charging solutions. With over 12 years of experience, we have established ourselves as a trusted brand in over 150 countries worldwide.

Our mission is to provide reliable, efficient, and innovative power solutions that enhance people's digital life. We believe that technology should be accessible to everyone, and our products are designed with this philosophy in mind.

Today, Luomas serves more than 50 million users globally, with a strong presence in North America, Europe, Asia, and emerging markets. Our commitment to quality and innovation has made us the preferred choice for consumers and businesses alike.
