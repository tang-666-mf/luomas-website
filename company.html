About Romoss

Powering the digital world since 2012

Our Story

Founded in 2012, Romoss has grown from a small startup to a global leader in mobile charging solutions. Headquartered in Shenzhen, China - the heart of the electronics industry - we have established ourselves as a trusted brand in over 150 countries.

Our mission is to empower people to stay connected wherever they go. We believe that reliable power should be accessible to everyone, everywhere. That's why we've dedicated ourselves to creating innovative, high-quality power banks that combine cutting-edge technology with user-centric design.

Today, Romoss serves over 50 million customers worldwide, with a product portfolio that includes everything from compact 5000mAh chargers to powerful 30000mAh portable power stations.
